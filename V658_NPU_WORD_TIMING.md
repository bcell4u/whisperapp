# v6.5.8: Parakeet NPU word timing and dual transcript playback

- v6.5.8 / versionCode 65008. No model downloads or changes to ORT-QNN model graphs.
- The TDT controller attaches its current encoder frame index to each emitted token (80 ms per frame). The pinned Metaspace/BPE tokenizer maps decoded words to those frame positions, including UTF-8 byte fallback. Timings are approximate decoder times, **not acoustic forced alignment**, and are never used to change the recognized words.
- The NPU worker returns `timed_words`, `timing_status`, and `timing_error` in the existing JSON IPC response. If timing fails, it still returns `ok=true` and the valid transcript. ASR/session diagnostics log timing counts and the bounded error string per chunk.
- The normal timeline sidecar keeps the existing `startMs/endMs/text` schema and now stores single-word cues for NPU when reconstruction and transcript-cleanup alignment pass. Speech-activity phrase timing remains the fallback; other ASR engines are unchanged.
- Regular playback and full transcript views already consume timeline cues, so they now highlight one NPU word at a time and seek by word, subject to the ~100 ms playback polling interval. A new speaker sidecar (`<recordingId>-speaker.json`) reuses those timestamps in the dedicated speaker menu and full speaker viewer, without changing the shared/transferred `.txt` files.
- Speaker attribution operates on word-sized native cues and groups consecutive same-speaker words only for readable speaker text. Its minimum evidence scales down for short timed words; ambiguous words remain `Unknown speaker`. Long-form speaker-window stitching stays unchanged.
- Speaker heading and optional `[HH:MM:SS]` time markers are excluded from clickable/highlightable word mapping. Existing sidecars without native words retain phrase-level playback. Re-transcribe the audio with v6.5.8 and regenerate the speaker transcript to populate word cues.
- Diagnostics include `timingStatus`, `timedWords`, `timingError`, `npu word-sync ... mode=native|estimated-fallback`, speaker attribution totals and speaker sidecar/viewer failures. No transcript contents or audio are logged. Cancellation, native worker failures, and silence handling remain unchanged.
- Verification: pure Kotlin standalone decoder/tokenizer/timeline/speaker tests, project source guards, ZIP/patch equality. No Android SDK, real QNN device, or end-to-end APK test available here. Native TDT timestamps and speaker boundaries must be compared against real interview audio.
